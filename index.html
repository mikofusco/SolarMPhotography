About the Photographer

Hi, I'm Miko.

I'm a video producer and photographer with over 10 years of experience in visual media. I've lived and worked in New York City, as well as Seoul, South Korea — two cities that shaped how I see light, people, and story.

Working with outlets such as EnVi Media and NABI Magazine, I've taken on a wide range of photo work, from editorial campaigns to intimate portrait sessions. Now, I'm ready to set up shop myself and work with you directly.

  • Professional headshots for LinkedIn, branding, and casting
  • Korean-style portrait sessions inspired by Seoul's studio aesthetic
  • Editorial and campaign photography for brands and publications
  • A decade of visual media experience across two continents
